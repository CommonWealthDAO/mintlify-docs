<Info>
  **Quick answer:** Download the WLTH app for iOS or Android, create an account, verify email, complete identity verification, then buy a first Slice from \$20 in USDC. Referral rewards require the app, not the web. For invite codes and points, see [Referrals](/referrals).
</Info>

WLTH runs on mobile. If someone sent a referral link, open it on the phone and it will take you straight to the download.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the WLTH app">
    Available on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/wlth/id6752845168) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=xyz.wlth.app). If someone sent a referral link, open it on the phone and it will take you straight to the download.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Enter your email address and confirm you agree to the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox and tap the confirmation link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete identity verification">
    Required before your first purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a wallet (optional)">
    You can connect any WalletConnect-compatible wallet, or use the embedded wallet created with your account. You can skip this and add one later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy your first Slice">
    Minimum \$20, settled in USDC. Your Slice appears in your Portfolio immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can also create an account at [wlth.xyz](https://wlth.xyz) on desktop. Web accounts work for browsing and purchasing, but referral rewards require the app. If you were referred by a friend, use the app or neither of you will be credited. See [Referrals](/referrals).
</Note>

Need help? Raise a request at [app.wlth.xyz/support](https://app.wlth.xyz/support). See [Contact support](/support/support-contact).
