WLTH builds private markets infrastructure. For too long, the biggest opportunities (pre-IPO equity, early-stage funds, and automated strategies) were locked behind gatekeepers and high minimums. WLTH opens fractional access starting from as little as **\$20** for eligible users outside the US and UK.

<Info>
  **What is WLTH?** In the app, eligible users outside the US and UK can buy **Slices**: tokenised economic exposure to WLTH allocations in private companies, recorded on Base as ERC-721 tokens, plus curated funds and gamified **Earn to Own** campaigns.
</Info>

## What you can do on WLTH

- **Invest** in pre-IPO companies and curated Web3 funds via Slices
- **Trade** Slices in the WLTH app for secondary liquidity when fund terms allow
- **Earn** tokenized equity allocations through Earn to Own missions
- **Participate** in the WLTH token economy, staking, and governance
