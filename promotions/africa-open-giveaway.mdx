WLTH is at Africa Open. One person who downloads the WLTH app and creates an account with the invite link from Africa Open wins $200 in USDC, sent straight to their WLTH app wallet.

## How to enter

1. Scan the QR code on the WLTH banner at Africa Open, or open [app.wlth.xyz/invite/021021](https://app.wlth.xyz/invite/021021).
2. Download the WLTH app or sign up through that link, so the invite code 021021 is attached to the new account.
3. That is the entry. No purchase, deposit or identity verification is needed to enter or win.

Only accounts created with invite code 021021 count. Accounts created without the code, or with a different code, are not entered.

## Dates

- Entry period: 00:00 on 1 December 2026 to 23:59 on 6 December 2026, South Africa Standard Time (SAST, UTC+2).
- The draw happens after the entry period closes.

## Prize

- One winner receives $200 in USDC, sent to the WLTH app wallet linked to their account.
- The winner is emailed at the address on their WLTH account to say the funds are already in their wallet. No reply or claim is needed.
- The prize can be used to invest in private market deals on WLTH. Investing is optional and is not a condition of the prize.

To enter, a person must:

- Be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority where they live, whichever is older.

- Be eligible to use WLTH under the [WLTH Terms & Conditions](/disclaimer/terms-and-conditions).

Employees of WLTH and its affiliated companies, Africa Open organisers and partners, and their immediate families, cannot enter.
