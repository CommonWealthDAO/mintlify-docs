# SpaceX referrals giveaway (ended)

### What the giveaway was

The referral giveaway rewarded users for inviting others to WLTH.

When someone referred bought a Slice, both the referrer and the referred person received a free SpaceX Slice.

This applied to both new and existing WLTH users.

***

### How the referral giveaway worked

1. A WLTH referral link was shared.
2. A new WLTH user signed up using that referral link.
3. The new user connected socials and verified the account.
4. They bought a Slice with a minimum purchase of $20.
5. They received a free $20 SpaceX Slice.
6. The referrer received a free $20 SpaceX Slice.

Each qualifying referral triggered one reward for each party.

***

### Who could take part

Eligible if:

* You were an existing or new WLTH user.
* You referred someone using a valid referral link.
* The referred user completed a qualifying Slice purchase during the campaign period.

Not eligible if:

* You attempted to refer yourself.
* The referral was flagged as invalid or abusive.
* The reward pool had already been exhausted.

***

### Minimum purchase

The minimum qualifying purchase was $20.

All Slice purchases met or exceeded this minimum by default.

***

### Reward

* The referred user received a free $20 SpaceX Slice.
* The referrer received a free $20 SpaceX Slice.

These rewards were promotional Slices issued as part of the referral campaign.

***

### How many rewards were available

The referral giveaway was capped at 500 SpaceX Slices in total.

Once all 500 reward Slices were issued, the campaign stopped automatically, even if a campaign end date had not yet been reached.

Rewards were allocated first come, first served.

***

### When rewards were sent

Rewards were sent automatically to qualifying users once the pool was depleted.

***

### Holding the Slice until delivery

Not required. As long as the Slice was purchased, it could be gifted or sold on the WLTH Marketplace.

***

### If a reward was not received

Common reasons included:

* The account was not created using a referral link.
* The reward pool had already been fully allocated.
* The purchase failed, was reversed, or refunded.
* The referral was identified as a self-referral.
* The referral was flagged as invalid or abusive.

If none of the above apply, raise a request at [app.wlth.xyz/support](https://app.wlth.xyz/support).

***

### Referring more than one person

Yes.

Multiple people could be referred during the campaign. Each successful referral that resulted in a qualifying purchase could earn a reward, subject to the overall reward cap.

***

### One trigger per referred user

No.

Each referred user could only trigger referral rewards once, regardless of how many purchases they made.

***

### Refunded purchases

If a qualifying purchase was refunded or reversed:

* Associated referral rewards could be revoked.
* This applied to both the referred user and the referrer.

***

### Guaranteed return or investment advice

No.

Referral rewards were promotional and did not imply guaranteed performance, returns, or outcomes. Capital is at risk.

***

### Campaign end

The referral giveaway ended when all 500 SpaceX reward Slices were allocated.

***

### Terms and conditions

Participation was subject to WLTH standard terms and campaign-specific conditions.
