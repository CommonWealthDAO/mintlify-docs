## Is WLTH licensed or regulated?

- WLTH Global Ltd is registered in ADGM and holds an ADGM licence for technology and platform infrastructure.
- WLTH Global Ltd is **not yet licensed in the UAE**; it is **in the licensing process** with the Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA). No regulated financial services are offered in or from ADGM today.
- WLTH is not registered with the US SEC or the UK FCA, and the WLTH app is **not available to US or UK persons** or residents of other restricted countries. See the [Disclaimer](/disclaimer).

We will update this page the day that changes. Until then, treat any message that says WLTH is "regulated by the FSRA" as false.

## What you own when you buy a Slice

A Slice is a token on Base (an ERC-721 token) that records your contractual economic right to part of a WLTH allocation in a private company.

- WLTH Corp. holds the underlying position. **You do not own shares** and you have no shareholder rights such as voting.
- Your claim is a contractual claim against WLTH Corp., not against the private company.
- When there is an exit event such as an IPO or acquisition, payouts settle in USDC under the terms of that offering.

Read [How WLTH Slices work](/investment/guides/how-wlth-slices-work) and the [Terms](/disclaimer/terms-and-conditions) before you invest.

## Things you should know before investing

- **Liquidity is not guaranteed.** You may be able to list a Slice for sale in the app, but there may be no buyer.
- **Private companies are risky.** They can fall in value, delay an IPO for years, or fail.
- **$WLTH is a separate token.** It is not a share in any WLTH company and is not backed by Slices or private company positions.

### How it works

1. Create an account in the app and pass the eligibility and identity checks.
2. Pick a company and buy Slices, from $20.
3. WLTH Corp. holds the underlying position. Your Slice records your economic share of it.
4. You can hold, or list your Slice for sale to other users in the app, subject to demand and the offering's terms.
5. On an exit event such as an IPO, payouts settle in USDC under the offering's terms.

More detail: [How WLTH Slices work](/investment/guides/how-wlth-slices-work) and [From Slice to public asset](/investment/guides/from-slice-to-public-asset).

### Costs

Fees are set per offering and shown before you confirm a purchase. Read the offering terms in the app.

### Pros

- Small minimum ($20) for companies that are usually hard to reach.
- On-chain record on Base; proof-of-ownership documents published for most listings ([see list](#proof-of-ownership)).
- Independent smart-contract audits by Hacken.
- iOS, Android and web apps.

### Cons and risks

- **Not yet licensed in the UAE.** No regulated financial services are offered in or from ADGM today.
- **You do not own shares.** A Slice is a contractual claim against WLTH Corp., so you also carry WLTH's own counterparty risk.
- **Liquidity is not guaranteed.** There may be no buyer when you want to sell.
- **No insurance** on user balances or Slices.
- **Not available** to US or UK persons.
- Private companies can lose value or never list.

### Who WLTH suits

People outside the US and UK who understand private-company risk, want small positions in late-stage private companies, and are comfortable with USDC settlement and on-chain records. It does not suit anyone who needs their money back on a set date.
