# SpaceX bonus promotion (ended)

### What the promotion was

The SpaceX bonus promotion rewarded users for purchasing primary Slices on WLTH.

For every qualifying primary purchase of $20 or more, a $20 SpaceX bonus Slice was issued.

***

### How the promotion worked

1. A primary Slice was purchased with a minimum value of $20.
2. The purchase confirmed successfully.
3. That purchase qualified for a $20 SpaceX bonus Slice.
4. The process could be repeated up to 5 times per wallet.
5. The promotion ended when the $20,000 total SpaceX bonus pool was fully allocated.

Each qualifying purchase triggered one bonus reward, subject to the wallet cap and remaining pool.

***

### Who could take part

Eligible if:

* You were a WLTH user.
* You completed a qualifying primary Slice purchase of $20 or more.
* The global bonus pool had not yet been fully allocated.
* You had not exceeded the 5 bonus limit per wallet.

Not eligible if:

* The $20,000 bonus pool had already been exhausted.
* The purchase failed, was reversed, or refunded.
* The account was flagged for abusive or invalid activity.

***

### Minimum purchase

The minimum qualifying purchase was $20.

Only primary Slice purchases qualified.

Secondary marketplace purchases did not qualify.

***

### Reward

A promotional $20 SpaceX Slice was issued for each qualifying primary purchase.

Each wallet could receive a maximum of 5 bonus rewards during the campaign.

***

### How many rewards were available

The promotion was capped at $20,000 in total SpaceX bonus allocation.

Once the full $20,000 had been distributed, the promotion ended automatically.

Rewards were allocated first come, first served.

***

### When rewards were sent

Bonus rewards were distributed every 24 hours.

If a qualifying primary purchase completed, the $20 SpaceX bonus Slice was sent to the WLTH account in the next 24 hour distribution cycle.

***

### Holding the Slice until delivery

Not required.

As long as the qualifying purchase completed, the purchased Slice could be gifted or sold on the WLTH Marketplace before the bonus was distributed.

***

### If a reward was not received

Common reasons included:

* The $20,000 bonus pool was fully allocated before the purchase confirmed.
* The 5 bonus limit per wallet had already been reached.
* The purchase failed, was reversed, or refunded.
* The account was flagged for invalid or abusive behaviour.

If none of the above apply, raise a request at [app.wlth.xyz/support](https://app.wlth.xyz/support).

***

### Guaranteed return or investment advice

No.

Bonus rewards were promotional and did not imply guaranteed performance, returns, or outcomes. Capital is at risk.

***

### Campaign end condition

The promotion ended automatically when the full $20,000 SpaceX bonus pool was allocated.
