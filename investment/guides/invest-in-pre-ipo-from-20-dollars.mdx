<Info>
  **Quick answer:** On WLTH you buy **Slices** (tokenized, 1:1 equity-backed positions) in pre-IPO companies from a **$20 minimum** per position. US accredited-investor status is not required for standard retail access, subject to jurisdiction and fund checks in the app. Traditional private-market platforms often start at **$10,000+**.
</Info>

WLTH turns a private-market allocation into a fractional Slice you can hold in a wallet on **Base**. The published minimum is **$20 per position**, the same figure used across the [pre-IPO investing](/investment/guides/what-is-pre-ipo-investing) and [non-accredited access](/investment/guides/pre-ipo-for-non-accredited-investors) guides.

## What $20 buys

A **Slice** is an ERC-721 NFT. It is tokenized economic exposure to a WLTH deal, backed 1:1 by equity held in a separate legal structure. It is not direct stock and does not confer shareholder voting rights in the target company. See [How WLTH Slices work](/investment/guides/how-wlth-slices-work).

| Topic | On WLTH |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum | **$20** per position |
| Accreditation | Not required for standard US retail access (jurisdiction and fund rules still apply) |
| Backing | 1:1 equity-backed Slices |
| Network | Base (Ethereum L2) |
| Liquidity | Conditional marketplace listing when fund terms and a buyer allow. Not guaranteed |

Typical tickets on platforms such as EquityZen, Forge, Hiive, and Linqto are often **$10,000+** and many require accredited investors. Compare platforms in [Pre-IPO platforms compared](/investment/guides/pre-ipo-platforms-compared).

## How to invest

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Sign up at [app.wlth.xyz](https://app.wlth.xyz).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete checks">
    Complete verification when prompted. Some jurisdictions are restricted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse offerings">
    Open [Pre-IPO Access](https://app.wlth.xyz/companies) and read the fund terms for the name you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy from $20">
    Choose an amount of at least **$20** and confirm. The Slice appears in **Portfolio**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Fees on the app are a **20% carry on profits** and a **5%** fee on secondary marketplace sales. See [Fees](/investment/staking-and-fees).

## After you hold a Slice

- **Hold** until a liquidity event, or list on the marketplace when eligible.
- After an IPO and unlock, claim the Slice to **USDC** from Portfolio. See [From Slice to public asset](/investment/guides/from-slice-to-public-asset).
